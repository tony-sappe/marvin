# Doctrine (sources)

Short pointers. Not quotes. Not essays.

- Beck (YAGNI, TDD) — ship the need; red-green for new behavior
- Hickey (simple vs complected) — prefer disentangled designs
- Ousterhout (deep modules) — small interface, rich behavior
- Feathers (characterization) — pin behavior before changing legacy
- Fowler (refactoring) — behavior-preserving steps
- Hunt & Thomas (DRY) — abstract on the third copy, or when one invariant has two owners
- Gall's Law — working complex systems evolve from working simple ones
- Chesterton's fence — understand a part before removing it
- RFC 2119 — MUST / SHOULD / MAY in contracts
- OpenAI harness engineering — spec as system of record; AGENTS.md as map
- Ponytail ladder — stop at the first rung that holds; keep the safety floor
- GitHub Spec Kit — specify before implement; constitution as durable constraints
- sudoconnor first-principles + repo-simplification — lifecycle surface area; pay rent; raptor-style subtract
- Meyer Design by Contract — preconditions, postconditions, invariants (one check, one place)
- Zeller scientific debugging — hypothesis, prediction, one experiment, logbook
- Google Testing Blog / SWE Book — blast radius and small/medium/large tests; Beyoncé rule
